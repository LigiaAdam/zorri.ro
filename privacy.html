Privacy

Privacy at zorri.ro

Short version: no ads, no tracking across other sites, and Google Analytics only if you say yes.

Who is responsible

ZORRI S.R.L., Bucharest, Romania (CUI 55137499, J2026042991009) is the controller for personal data collected through zorri.ro. Write to hello@zorri.ro for anything on this page.

This page covers zorri.ro only. ReLoop.me, ZORRI's capability intelligence platform, has its own privacy policy.

Analytics, only with your consent

When you first visit, a banner asks whether we may use Google Analytics. Until you click Accept, no analytics script is loaded and no cookies are set. If you click Decline, nothing is loaded and your choice is remembered in your browser.

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 records which pages you view, how you arrived (for example from LinkedIn or a search engine), your device and browser type and your approximate location (country or city). Advertising features and ad personalisation are switched off. We use this only to understand which pages are useful.

Cookies set after you accept: _ga and _ga_HQ5BXEPBLE (Google Analytics, up to 2 years). Your choice itself is stored as zorri-consent in your browser's local storage, not as a cookie.

You can change your mind at any time through Cookie settings in the footer of every page. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).

Google Analytics is provided by Google Ireland Limited. Data may be processed in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and, if we work together, to scope and deliver the work. Legal basis: steps you ask for before a contract and our legitimate interest in answering (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Email runs on Google Workspace.

We keep correspondence as long as the conversation or the work needs it, and longer only where Romanian accounting or tax law requires it. We never sell it or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Hosting

zorri.ro is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages and may keep it in security logs. Fonts are served from zorri.ro itself, not from a third-party font service.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to hello@zorri.ro; we reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).
