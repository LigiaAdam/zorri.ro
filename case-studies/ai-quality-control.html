How to stop wrong AI answers before they reach users

Automated, fail-closed quality control for ReLoop.me's AI-generated reports.

ZORRI built a production AI pipeline for ReLoop.me where every report passes rule checks and an independent AI review before delivery. A report that fails is held and the owner is alerted. Users never receive raw model output.

Published 23 November 2026 · Figures from the live engine as of 23 November 2026 · By Ligia Adam

The challenge: “mostly right” isn't good enough

ReLoop.me is a capability intelligence platform for nonlinear careers and skills-based hiring. It reads the professional capability inside a CV or job description: what is claimed versus what is demonstrated, the skills mix the evidence supports, and an estimated market value for the role or profile. It reads the way an experienced recruiter and domain specialist would, and gives a neutral, calibrated view of what is actually there.

A user submits a CV or job description. ReLoop.me runs it through a multi-step, rules-enforced AI engine and emails a structured report: Profile Intel for a CV, Role Intel for a job description.

People use these reports for career and salary decisions, so a wrong or contradictory report does more damage than a late one. Large language models are probabilistic. They can return malformed output, drop required sections, or contradict their own assessment. Raw output couldn't be sent to users unchecked, and doing every check by hand wouldn't scale.

What we built

  • Rule checks on every read before delivery. They validate scoring-band logic, the claims-versus-evidence audit and the report structure.
  • A separate AI reviewer. A model call isolated from the one that wrote the read scores the draft and returns specific corrections with its reasoning. The corrections are merged before delivery.
  • A fail-closed delivery gate. If a check fails, or the reviewer returns no verdict, the report is held, the owner is alerted, and nothing reaches the user.
  • Input guards (Profile Intel). A cost guard and an empty-input check stop broken or blank uploads before any API tokens are spent.
  • A non-blocking end-of-run check (Profile Intel). If an internal step fails after a valid report has already gone out, the owner is alerted without holding back a report that passed every gate.
  • Versioned nodes with rollback. Every production change records the version it replaces, so it can be reversed straight away if quality drops.

Result

Nothing in ReLoop.me ships as raw model output, and every production change has a named rollback path. Figures from the live engine, as of 23 November 2026:

  • 573reads produced, 14 June to 21 November 2026: 324 Role Intel and 249 Profile Intel, test runs included
  • 12Role Intel reads held by the gate and logged, 3 October to 12 November 2026, each one stopped before anything was stored or emailed
  • 34% → 4%spread of the pay estimate on the same CV across repeat runs, before and after a stability fix (synthetic CVs, 21 November 2026)

In one case found in testing, the reviewer step broke on quoted text in a German job ad. The gate held the read instead of sending a broken one, and the fix was proved on a staging copy of the workflow before release.

The checks catch structural and logic errors. They don't make a model infallible. That's why held reports go to a person and every change can be rolled back.
