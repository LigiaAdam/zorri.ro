How to stop wrong AI answers before they reach users

Automated, fail-closed quality control for ReLoop.me's AI-generated reports.

The challenge: AI getting it “mostly right” isn't good enough

ReLoop.me is an AI-powered platform that reads the actual professional capability inside a CV or a job description, in perspective and in between the lines, to give you the bigger picture:

  • claims versus what's actually demonstrated
  • the specific skills combo-mix evidenced by the data submitted
  • the estimated market value for a specific role or profile

It's built to follow the human logic of an experienced recruiter or domain specialist, to give a neutral, AI-scaled perspective of what's truly there skills-wise, across domains and geographies.

Designed specifically to handle the complexity inside nonlinear careers, ReLoop.me is a capability intelligence platform that supports fair reviews and skills-based hiring.

How it works

  • A user submits a CV or job description
  • →
  • a multi-step, rules-enforced AI engine reads it
  • →
  • a structured report is emailed

Profile Intel for a CV, Role Intel for a job description.

People and companies use these reports for career and salary decisions.

A wrong or contradictory report does more damage than a late one.

Large language models are probabilistic. They can return malformed output, drop required sections, or contradict their own assessment.

Raw output couldn't be sent to users unchecked, and doing every check by hand wouldn't scale.

What we built

Checking what comes in: the input guards

AI feeds on the data it receives, but even AI can fail to read some data.

The input guards, a cost guard and an empty-input check, stop broken or blank uploads before any API tokens are spent. (Live on Profile Intel.)

Checking what is in: the rule checks

The rules validate the scoring-band logic, the claims-versus-evidence audit and the report structure.

Each rule was added specifically to better steer AI into getting it right, every time.

The smart re-check: a distinct AI reviewer of the AI layer before it

A separate AI, with no memory of the one that wrote the read, scores the draft and returns specific corrections, with its reasoning.

The corrections are merged and enforced in the following layers, before a report is actually delivered.

The internal safe space for failure: if AI fails, delivery is blocked

If one of the checks along the route fails, for any reason, or if the AI reviewer returns no verdict on key aspects, the report is held internally.

The owner is alerted that the report needs manual review, so the human in the loop steps in, and nothing reaches the user.

In AI engineering, this is called a fail-closed delivery gate.

Continuous improvement: versioned nodes with rollback

Each new rule, new blocker or new drift is recorded and fixed, so the engine is continuously updated.

Every production change records the version it replaces, so it can be reversed straight away if quality drops.

The tilts

Like a ReLoop.me report, the engine leans a certain way on purpose. Each lean, and why:

  • Late over wrongA held report costs a few hours. A wrong one costs a career or salary decision, and the user's trust.
  • A person over a retry loopWhen a read fails, the owner looks at it. Re-running until something passes would hide the reason it failed.
  • Rollback over a hotfixEvery change can be reversed straight away, so a bad release is undone first and fixed second.

Result

Nothing in ReLoop.me ships as raw AI output, and every production change has a named rollback path.

Figures from the live engine, as of 23 November 2026:

  • 573reads produced, 14 June to 21 November 2026: 324 Role Intel and 249 Profile Intel, test runs included
  • 12Role Intel reads held by the gate and logged, 3 October to 12 November 2026, each one stopped before anything was stored or emailed
  • 34% → 4%spread of the pay estimate on the same CV across repeat runs, before and after a stability fix (synthetic CVs, 21 November 2026)

How this works in action

Once, the reviewer step broke on quoted text in a German job ad.

The gate held the read instead of sending a broken one, and the fix was proved on a staging copy of the workflow before release.

The checks also catch structural and logic errors. They don't make AI infallible.

That's why held reports go to a person, and every change can be rolled back.
