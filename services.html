Working with ZORRI

How do I start?

Email a few lines about what you need, or the name of a service. You get a reply within 2 working days and a free 30-minute call, then a written scope and quote. Work starts once you confirm.

How is an engagement quoted?

Every engagement is scoped and quoted in writing before work starts. ZORRI is not a VAT payer, so quotes are final, with no VAT added.

Can we start small?

Yes. Many projects start with a focused engagement, such as an audit or a research brief, and continue into a sprint or an ongoing partnership once the scope is clear.

Is the governance and pay transparency work legal advice?

No. It is operational readiness: classification, gaps and what to fix. Where you need a legal opinion, we work alongside your lawyers.

Do you work outside Romania?

Yes. ZORRI works with clients across the European Union, in English and Romanian, remotely or in person, based on availability.
