Practice

AI orchestration

From AI agents to deterministic workflows.

We build AI agents, pipelines, automation and custom software, from autonomous agents where judgement helps to deterministic workflows where every run must match. Every step is versioned, every output checked, every decision traceable. Then we map them against the EU AI Act and GDPR, so they can be defended as well as used.

AI that holds up under scrutiny

An agent or a workflow holds up under scrutiny when it behaves predictably within the limits you set, stops itself when an output fails a check, and leaves a record of why. Where you need the same answer to the same input every time, we make it deterministic. That is engineering and governance together, and it is what this practice delivers.

Questions

What is AI orchestration?

AI orchestration is the design of a workflow in which one or more AI models, data sources and tools run in a defined order, with checks between the steps. The goal is output you can trust and trace, not a single clever prompt.

What does “fails closed” mean?

If an output does not pass its checks, the workflow stops instead of passing it on. A wrong answer is held back rather than shipped.

Is the AI Governance Review legal advice?

No. It is a technical and operational review: risk classification, your role under the EU AI Act, documentation gaps and design fixes. Your lawyer can work from it.

Which tools do you build with?

Large language models (including the Claude API), n8n for orchestration, and integrations with the systems you already use. The choice is agreed during scoping.
