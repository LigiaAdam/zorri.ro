Questions

What is market intelligence?

Market intelligence is structured evidence about a market — buyers, competitors, prices, labour and regulation — collected and analysed so a decision can be made on it. We deliver it as a written brief with its sources.

Is pay transparency work legal advice?

No. We prepare the operational material: audited job ads, written pay-range reasoning and a gap list against Directive (EU) 2023/970. Your lawyer or HR counsel can work from it.

Do you guarantee that a funding application wins?

No. Funding decisions belong to the evaluators. We make sure the application is eligible, coherent, complete and on time.

Which funding programmes do you work with?

EU programmes (such as Horizon Europe, the EIC and Digital Europe) and Romanian programmes financed from EU funds. Eligibility is checked against the specific call before any work starts.

How is an engagement quoted?

Every engagement is scoped and quoted in writing before work starts. ZORRI is not a VAT payer, so quotes are final, with no VAT added.

Need evidence before a decision?

Tell us the decision and the deadline. You'll get a scope and a date for the readout.
