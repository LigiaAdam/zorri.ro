Making complex products legible

Technical products rarely fail for lack of capability. They fail because buyers can't tell what they are or why they matter. This practice makes the product legible: a category it belongs to, a position it can defend, and proof buyers trust.

Questions

What is go-to-market strategy?

Go-to-market strategy is the plan for how a product reaches and wins its buyers: who they are, what problem it solves for them, how it is positioned and priced, and which channels carry it.

What is GEO?

GEO, generative engine optimisation, is the work of making sure AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI answers find, understand and correctly describe your company.
