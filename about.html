About

Ligia Adam

Founder of ZORRI. Builder, then operator.

Ligia has spent more than fifteen years making complex technology legible to the market, in cybersecurity, enterprise hardware, edtech, civic tech, energy and now AI. She founded ZORRI in 2026 and built its product, ReLoop.me, end to end.
