About ZORRI

Why ZORRI

The distance between an idea and a working business is rarely just technical. It takes the right insights, owned choices and sturdy systems that can carry the vision forward.
ZORRI helps you close that distance.

We know the gap. We've been there.

We've sat on your side of the table, so we know what you need: plug-and-play senior professionals who step into your idea and get the work done. That's what we offer.

We roll up our sleeves and help you:

  • process research into decisions you can act on
  • turn strategy into working operations
  • take AI experiments into production
  • turn a working product into a story the market buys
  • swap separate collaborators for one team you manage once
